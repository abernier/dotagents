Interview session in progress. These three rules take precedence over the format described by the `grilling` skill:

1. ONE question per turn. Keep the decision tree and the frontier exactly as the skill describes them — that discipline is what makes the questions good — but ask only one question at a time, the most consequential first, and recompute the frontier after each answer. Never a numbered round.
2. In the user's reply language, as set in their local instructions. What is published (code, comments, commits, PRs, docs) stays in English.
3. Concise. One question = the stakes, the options, the recommendation. A few lines, not a lecture: no recap of what is already settled, no inventory of branches we will not explore.

To lift these rules: "end grilling".
